About Us

Hiranya Seranika

Founder & Creative Director

Hiranya Seranika founded eNVision with a simple belief: eyewear should do more than improve vision—it should enhance confidence and personal style. Inspired by her passion for fashion and design, she noticed that many eyewear options were either stylish but uncomfortable, or comfortable but lacking character.

Determined to bridge that gap, Hira created eNVision to offer premium eyewear that combines quality craftsmanship, timeless aesthetics, and everyday comfort. Every collection is carefully curated to help customers express themselves while enjoying frames designed to last.

Our Story

Founded in 2021, eNVision began with a mission to make premium eyewear more accessible without compromising on quality or design. What started as a small idea quickly grew into a brand dedicated to helping people see clearly while looking their best.

Our Philosophy

At eNVision, we believe that great eyewear is a balance of style, comfort, and functionality. Every frame is selected with attention to detail, ensuring that customers receive products that not only look exceptional but also fit seamlessly into their daily lives.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted eyewear brand that empowers individuals to express themselves confidently. We aim to continuously innovate our collections while maintaining our commitment to quality, affordability, and customer satisfaction.
